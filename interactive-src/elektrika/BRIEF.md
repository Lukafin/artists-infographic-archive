# Elektrika — sklenjeni tokokrog

Anatomy Figure: isolated DC laboratory circuit, companion to Elektrika.png (18 June 2026, Slovenian, original assignment 256deb5c-62e8-4d2d-b6c9-c9f11bdd9ca5). The original poster remains unchanged.

Truth model: I = U/R only with switch closed. P = U·I. Voltage 0–12 V, ohmic resistance 4–24 Ω. Ideal source, wires and ammeter; no temperature dependence, electron drift velocity or transient response. Moving dots show conventional current from + through the external circuit to −, with illustrative speed proportional to I. Changing U from 6 to 12 at 12 Ω gives twice the current and four times power. Changing R from 12 to 24 at 6 V halves current and power.

Purposeful parts: 1 mounting plate; 4 pairs of rubber feet; 8 insulating conductor supports and 8 copper bars meeting flush at corners; 4 device enclosures and 4 covers; 16 fasteners; 4 dial bodies and 4 rims. 57 solids, plus rooted engraved scales, terminal labels, needles and current markers. Four devices: source at left, rotary switch at back, resistor at right, series ammeter at front. True-depth ordering from Anatomy recorder; no cables or explode mode.
